# Dream Home

A browser-based personal 3D retreat. The visitor stands inside one open seaside loft with a gaming workspace, a large sheltered bed, a reading chair by the sea window, and an upstairs Chinese-inspired tea room. It is a place to be in, with diorama views kept as a second way of looking; it is not a room planner or construction drawing.

## Platform

Web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, Three.js, React Three Fiber, Drei, CSS. No backend or paid APIs.

## Required behavior

First-person presence: look around, walk the floor and the stair, sit at the desk, on the bed, in the reading chair and at the tea table; orbit and zoom from the two outside views; configurable website through the computer; clickable bookcases, tea table, and rolling TV; animated curtains; lamps; day, sunset, night, and rain; responsive keyboard-accessible controls; capped DPR and paused hidden-page rendering.

The room also keeps a second warm corner and a few places to settle that are not on the toolbar: a wood stove whose fire never goes out, with the cat asleep beside it; a bench built into the window sill that can be curled up on; a string of bulbs along the loft railing; a pouf and magazines by the reading chair; and a blanket ladder on the bed screen.

Smaller interactions give the room things to find: dresser drawers that slide open, a sea-window casement that swings out and sets the curtains moving, a teapot that pours, single book spines that open the reading list at their title, the coat and hat by the door that stand the visitor at the dresser mirror, a record player whose ambience is synthesised in the browser rather than downloaded, and a meteor over the sea when the window is opened after dark. The chosen light, lamps and curtains are remembered between visits.

## Design direction

The user specified a warm ocean loft with a full-height timber-framed sea window. The room is a wide rectangle with a compact, deeper tea-room mezzanine along the rear wall and a short wing over the bed head. The first floor includes a deep left-front gaming desk whose chair backs toward the solid left wall, and a right-rear bed whose head meets the rear wall under a lowered linen canopy at mezzanine height, screened from the room and open to the sea. A straight stair runs up the left wall beside the loft's short edge and lands in the rear-left corner, so the aisle in front of the loft stays open. A dresser stands under the right end of the loft. Bookcases on both floors connect the tea room and the lower stair area. All spaces should read together from the doorway and from the outside views, with a clear route and few supports. The room should feel warm and lived-in through light, material and a few personal traces, without clutter.
